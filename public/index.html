Divisions

Teams comes from field slots and coaches you have; capacity is teams × roster size. SE program words are matched against the program name a family picks in SportsEngine (comma-separated, e.g. minors, minor league). Ages are league ages, used to age last season's kids up and to flag kids registered in the wrong division. Draft board relays that division's registrations to the Majors draft board.

Last season's rosters

Paste from a spreadsheet or CSV with a header row: Name (or First / Last), Birthdate, Division, Team. Optional Next Division overrides placement. Each kid becomes an Expected returner holding a seat, placed by league age (staying put while still eligible), else in the same division. Kids older than every division are skipped.
